03 — The Artist

Kopano Madisa

Kopano Madisa works across mixed media, photography and digital design. Their practice is rooted in identity and heritage — exploring how the self is carried, altered and remembered across generations.

This body of work gathers pieces made between 2021 and 2025, from early A4 mixed-media studies to a completed A2 photographic series and a pair of digital posters titled Morwadiwa Madisa.
